# Privacy Policy for Giỏ Từ

**Effective Date:** November 14, 2026

## 1. Data Collection and Usage

**Giỏ Từ** (the "Extension") is designed to help users save and manage vocabulary directly within their browser. The Extension operates entirely on your local machine. We do not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell your personal data, vocabulary lists, or browsing history to any third-party servers.

## 2. Local Storage

All vocabulary, notes, and settings saved via the Extension are stored locally on your device using your browser's built-in local storage mechanisms (such as IndexedDB).

- You have full control over this data.
- You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser data or using the Extension's built-in delete functions.

## 3. Permissions Justification

To function properly, the Extension requires the following permissions:

- **`contextMenus`:** Used to provide a convenient right-click menu option, allowing you to quickly save highlighted text as vocabulary.
- **`storage`:** Used strictly to save your vocabulary list and settings locally on your machine.
- **Host permissions (Webpage access):** Used to capture the specific text you highlight on active web pages so it can be saved to your local list. The Extension does not monitor or track your general browsing activity.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The Extension does not use any third-party analytics or tracking services. Your data remains yours alone.

## 5. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

## 6. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
