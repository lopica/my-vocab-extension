Privacy Policy for Giỏ Từ

1. Data Collection and Usage
   Giỏ Từ (the "Extension") is designed to help users save and manage vocabulary directly within their browser. The Extension does not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell your personal data or browsing history to any third-party servers.

2. Local Storage
   All vocabulary, notes, and settings saved via the Extension are stored locally on your device using your browser's local storage (IndexedDB). You have full control over this data and can delete it at any time by clearing your browser data or using the extension's built-in delete functions.

3. Permissions

contextMenus: Used to provide a right-click menu option to quickly save highlighted text.

storage: Used to save your vocabulary list locally on your machine.

Host permissions (Webpage access): Used strictly to capture the text you highlight on active web pages to save it to your local list.

4. Changes to this Policy
   We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

5. Contact
   If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the email provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
